The tree follows the WAI-ARIA tree view pattern: roles, levels and states for screen readers, a single tab stop, and the keyboard to move around. Press Tab to reach the tree. Nothing is selected yet, so the first node is the tab stop. Press Enter to open a node. The panels on the right show what assistive technology sees.

Form

The keys only act on the tree when the focus is on a node, so Home, End, Enter and the arrow keys still work in these fields. Tab moves on to the tree.

Keyboard

TabMove the focus into or out of the tree. The tree is one tab stop.
↓Select the next visible node.
↑Select the previous visible node.
→Open a closed folder, or move to its first child.
←Close an open folder, or move to the parent.
HomeSelect the first node.
EndSelect the last visible node.
EnterOpen the node: dispatch tree.activate.
SpaceSelect the first node when nothing is selected.
